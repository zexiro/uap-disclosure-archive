Three teams of two federal law-enforcement special agents (USPER1 through USPER6) independently describe seeing orange orbs in the sky emit-launch smaller red orbs in groups of two to four, being the general consensus. This is stated to have occurred at least five times. Each time, a single orange orb would appear, launch red orbs, then disappear. The orange orb was only visible for one or two seconds; the red orbs would generally move away from the orange “mother” orb in a horizontal path. One witness stated the red orbs would “swoop down” after being launched from the larger orange orb.
